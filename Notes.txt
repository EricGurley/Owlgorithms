MATHEMATICS

1. Calculus 3 (Multivariable)

    Standard Textbook: Calculus: Early Transcendentals by James Stewart.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 12–16. Vectors and the Geometry of Space, Vector Functions, Partial Derivatives, Multiple Integrals, and Vector Calculus.

2. Differential Equations

    Standard Textbook: Elementary Differential Equations and Boundary Value Problems by William E. Boyce and Richard C. DiPrima.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–7. First-Order ODEs, Second-Order Linear Equations, Higher-Order Linear Equations, Series Solutions, Laplace Transforms, and Systems of First-Order Linear Equations.

3. Linear Algebra

    Standard Textbook: Linear Algebra and Its Applications by David C. Lay.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–7. Linear Equations in Linear Algebra, Matrix Algebra, Determinants, Vector Spaces, Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors, Orthogonality and Least Squares, and Symmetric Matrices.

4. Tensor Calculus

    Standard Textbook: A First Course in General Relativity by Bernard F. Schutz. 

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–4. Special Relativity, Vector Analysis, Tensor Analysis in Special Relativity, and Perfect Fluids/Intro to Curved Spacetime.




PHYSICS

1. Astrophysics I & II

    Standard Textbook: An Introduction to Modern Astrophysics (The "Big Orange Book") by Bradley W. Carroll and Dale A. Ostlie.

    Chapters Covered (Astro I): Chapters 1–17. Covers Celestial Mechanics, The Nature of Light, Stellar Interiors, The Sun, Stellar Evolution, White Dwarfs, Neutron Stars, and Black Holes.

    Chapters Covered (Astro II): Chapters 18–30. Covers Binary Systems, The Milky Way, Galactic Dynamics, Active Galaxies, and Cosmology.

2. University Physics III (Modern Physics)

    Standard Textbook: University Physics with Modern Physics by Hugh D. Young and Roger A. Freedman.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 37–44. Covers Special Relativity, Photons/Matter Waves, Quantum Mechanics Intro, Atomic Structure, Molecules and Solids, and Nuclear Physics.

3. Classical Mechanics (Hamiltonian and Lagrangian)

    Standard Textbook: Classical Mechanics by John R. Taylor.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–11, plus 13. Newton's Laws, Oscillations, Calculus of Variations, Lagrange's Equations, Central Forces, Non-inertial Frames, Rigid Bodies, Coupled Oscillators, and Hamiltonian Mechanics.

4. Special and General Relativity

    Standard Textbook: Spacetime and Geometry: An Introduction to General Relativity by Sean M. Carroll.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–7. Special Relativity/Flat Spacetime, Manifolds, Curvature, Gravitation (Einstein's Field Equations), The Schwarzschild Solution (Black Holes), and Cosmology.

5. Quantum Mechanics

    Standard Textbook: Introduction to Quantum Mechanics by David J. Griffiths.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–7. The Wave Function, The Time-Independent Schrödinger Equation, Formalism, Quantum Mechanics in Three Dimensions, Identical Particles, and Time-Independent Perturbation Theory.




ENGINEERING

1. Thermodynamics

    Standard Textbook: Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach by Yunus A. Çengel and Michael A. Boles.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–10. Basic concepts, energy analysis, properties of pure substances, closed systems, open systems, the second law of thermodynamics, entropy, and basic power cycles (like the Rankine and Brayton cycles).


2. Fluid Mechanics

    Standard Textbook: Fundamentals of Fluid Mechanics by Bruce R. Munson, Donald F. Young, and Theodore H. Okiishi.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–9. Fluid Statics, Elementary Fluid Dynamics (Bernoulli), Kinematics, Finite Control Volume Analysis, Differential Analysis (Navier-Stokes), Similitude, Viscous Pipe Flow, and Flow Over Immersed Bodies.

3. Signals and Systems

    Standard Textbook: Signals and Systems by Alan V. Oppenheim and Alan S. Willsky.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–10. Continuous and discrete LTI systems, Fourier series and transforms, filtering, sampling, the Laplace transform, and the Z-transform.

4. Electromagnetics

    Standard Textbook: Fundamentals of Applied Electromagnetics by Fawwaz T. Ulaby or Elements of Electromagnetics by Matthew N.O. Sadiku.

    Chapters Covered: Chapters 1–9. Vector Calculus, Electrostatics, Magnetostatics, Maxwell's Equations, Plane Waves, and Transmission Lines.
